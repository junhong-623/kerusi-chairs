# 庙会红椅 · Kenduri Red · Kerusi Kenduri Merah

Creator: Jee Production (junhong-623) — https://jeeprod.com

Original procedural model made with Codex assistance and refined by the creator.
Inspired by the familiar red plastic chairs at temple fairs, family gatherings
and Malaysian kenduri. The reference photograph was used for shape inspiration;
it is not included in this package. No third-party model, texture or brand label
is embedded in the GLB.

## Chair story

**中文**：庙会还没开场，它先到；kenduri 还没开饭，它先占位。
大人物坐过，小朋友站过，隔壁 uncle 还搬它去吹水。
没有沙发的排场，却有全 kampung 的出场经验。谁先搬到，谁先坐。

**English**: First at the temple fair, early for the kenduri, and somehow always
beside an uncle telling one more story. No sofa-level luxury lah—just a red
plastic legend with a whole kampung's worth of appearances. Grab a seat before
someone grabs the chair.

**Bahasa Melayu**: Kenduri belum mula, dia dah sampai. Makanan belum keluar,
dia dah cop tempat. Dari pesta tokong sampai gathering keluarga, kerusi merah
ini memang kaki lepak. Tak mewah macam sofa lah, tapi pengalaman satu kampung.
Siapa angkat dulu, dia duduk dulu.

## Model

- `red-kenduri-chair.glb`: one static chair mesh, upright, approximately metre scale.
- 88,404 triangles; 1,590,972 bytes (1.59 MB).
- Standard glTF PBR material with clearcoat; no external textures.
- Complete back and underside, outward-opening L-section legs, four seat
  recesses, connecting edges outside the legs, a curved backrest, mirrored
  D-shaped back openings and seat ventilation holes.
- `preview.png`, `rear.png`, `side.png`, `top.png`, `underside.png`: actual GLB renders.
- Desktop drag/zoom and mobile touch rotation checked in the keru.si viewer.
